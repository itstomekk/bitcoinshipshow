HOW TO USE IT
  - Keep the mark as it is: no recolouring, no drop shadows, no outlines, no
    rotation, no extra words locked into the logo.
  - Give it room. Keep clear space of at least half the boat's height around it.
  - On dark, use the cream or gold wordmark. On light, use ink.
  - Write the name "Bitcoin Shipshow" - "Shipshow" is one word. Episodes are
    E01, E02, ... The show is monthly, live, a pirates' community call.
  - Hashtag: #bitcoinshipshow (secondary: #shipshow)
  - X @bitcoinshipshow  |  YouTube @BitcoinShipshow
  - Nostr npub1mtjn0yp705hvgszssf2ddjpzk3zzjzgvu58dwwpkxqud0hde6fxss7cwyw
  - Times are given as 13:00 ET / 19:00 CET, never UTC.
  - The live room link is private: it goes out by email only. Link to
    https://bitcoinship.show/live/ instead.

THE LOOK (for anything you draw or generate)
  Hybrid paper-cut collage meets vintage woodcut poster: layered cut-paper
  shapes with visible torn edges, bold black woodcut outlines, limited ink
  palette, paper grain, mid-century print feel. Minimal detail, generous empty
  space. Palette: sea #061726, sea-mid #0C2E4A, sea-lite #14486F,
  parchment #CBDCE8, gold #D9AE3C. No neon, no glassmorphism, no 3D gloss,
  no rounded corners.
