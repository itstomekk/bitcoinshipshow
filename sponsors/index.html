The format

3
live project demos per episode
roughly an hour and a half, then the room stays open off the record

Each episode mixes early, unknown builders with one well-known project showing a fresh release, plus a short slot for one project that's still too early to demo. Mostly software, with room for art, community and business. Every episode is produced with a proper streaming setup and cut into quality video content — full episodes and clips — published across platforms. The private room is where the real networking happens — no recording, no clips.

What gets featured

Anything interesting being built on Bitcoin, Lightning, or Nostr. Think:

⚡ Lightning wallets & hubs 🟣 Nostr clients & tools 🔐 Hardware & signers 🎵 Music & media apps 🎬 Bitcoin films & art 🎓 Education projects 🌍 Meetups, hubs & circular economies 🛠️ Dev tools & open source

Why partner

Reach the builders

Your name in front of the people writing the code, shipping the hardware, and starting the companies — plus every featured project's community, brought to the room.

Nominate projects

Partners can put projects forward for a demo slot. Hosts curate for quality, so the show stays credible — and so does your association with it.

On-air recognition

Thanked in every episode and in published recordings. Prizes and giveaways can carry your name — in-kind contributions welcome.

Early discovery

See projects at ideation stage, before anyone else. The show is a live deal-flow and talent pipeline for the ecosystem.

Partners aren't only companies. We also team up with brands, YouTubers, podcasters, and influencers in the space — support can come as money, reach, prizes, or a project to nominate. If you bring an audience of builders, we should talk.

The show stays independent: hosts pick what's featured, and at least one demo slot every month goes to a project with no partner attached. That independence is what makes the audience trust it — and what makes partnering worth it.

What a partner season looks like

Founding season open
  • 3–4 partners per season, so recognition isn't diluted
  • Project nomination rights each episode
  • Thank-you on air and in every published episode and clip
  • A seat in the private off-record room

Partnerships run by season, and we keep the number small on purpose. Founding partners get in before the format is proven and are credited as such for as long as the show runs.

Support doesn't have to be cash — reach, prizes or hardware work too. Tell us what you'd want out of it and we'll figure out the shape.

What partners cover is production and operations. This is a community show, not a media business — audience zaps stay value-for-value.

Who's behind it

A team of hosts who've run Bitcoin community calls before and know what kept people coming back: the room, not the product. This is a grassroots community show — promoted through each host's personal network and the featured projects' own communities, tied to no single company, spread by hashtag and word of mouth rather than a marketing machine.
